## Commercial PMS — Global

- [Oracle OPERA Cloud](https://www.oracle.com/hospitality/hotel-property-management/hotel-pms-software/) — The enterprise standard for chains: cloud PMS with deep integrations and open APIs.
- [Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com) — All-in-one cloud PMS, channel manager, and booking engine; popular with independents, hostels, and groups.
- [Mews](https://www.mews.com) — Cloud-native PMS with an open API marketplace; strong in Europe and expanding globally.
- [RoomRaccoon](https://www.roomraccoon.com) — All-in-one hotel management software for independents: PMS, channel manager, booking engine, and payments.
- [Guesty](https://www.guesty.com) — PMS purpose-built for short-term rentals and vacation-rental managers.

## Commercial PMS — India

- [RoomNexa](https://www.roomnexa.com) — Cloud PMS for independent hotels, GST-ready billing, free plan available.
- [Hotelogix](https://www.hotelogix.com) — India-built cloud PMS for small and mid-size hotels, widely used across Asia.
- [eZee Absolute (Yanolja Cloud)](https://www.ezeetechnosys.com) — Long-running budget PMS brand, now part of Yanolja Cloud.
- [DJUBO](https://www.djubo.com) — Indian hotel-tech suite: PMS, channel manager, and booking engine.
- [Aiosell](https://www.aiosell.com) — All-in-one PMS with strong revenue-management tools and transparent pricing.
- [STAAH](https://www.staah.com) — NZ-founded platform with deep India presence: channel manager and booking engine suite.

## Channel Managers

- [SiteMinder](https://www.siteminder.com) — The world's leading hotel commerce platform: channel manager plus booking engine, used by 56,000+ hotels.
- [Beds24](https://www.beds24.com) — Channel manager with a developer-friendly API; a favorite of tech-savvy independents.
- [Beds24 Platform SDK](https://github.com/Sleepy-beds/beds24-platform) — Typed, dependency-free SDK for the Beds24 channel manager API.

## Booking Engines & Direct Booking Tools

- [Hotelchamp](https://www.hotelchamp.com) — Direct-booking tools and website personalization that convert lookers into bookers.
- [The Hotels Network](https://www.thehotelsnetwork.com) — Direct booking technology: personalization, price comparison, and conversion tools.
- [Amadeus Hotel Booking (Django demo)](https://github.com/amadeus4dev/amadeus-hotel-booking-django) — Demo hotel booking engine using the Amadeus Self-Service API (Python/Django; archived but instructive).

## Revenue Management & Rate Shopping

- [PriceLabs](https://www.pricelabs.co) — Dynamic pricing for hotels and vacation rentals; automates daily rate adjustments.
- [Beyond](https://www.beyondpricing.com) — Revenue management with real-time market data for short-term rentals.
- [Atomize](https://www.atomize.com) — Automated revenue management driven by real-time demand data.

## Point of Sale (POS)

- [Toast](https://www.toasttab.com) — Restaurant POS widely used in hospitality: payments, online ordering, and payroll.
- [Petpooja](https://www.petpooja.com) — India's leading restaurant POS: billing, inventory, and online-order aggregation.

## Guest Messaging & CRM

- [Revinate](https://www.revinate.com) — Guest data platform for 12,500+ hotels: email marketing, messaging, and reputation.
- [Akia](https://www.akia.com) — Guest messaging automation with AI-powered chat across SMS, WhatsApp, and web.

## Review & Reputation Management

- [TrustYou](https://www.trustyou.com) — Guest feedback platform: review aggregation, surveys, and messaging.
- [ReviewPro](https://www.reviewpro.com) — Online reputation management for hotels (part of Shiji Group).

## Housekeeping & Operations

- [Flexkeeping](https://www.flexkeeping.com) — Housekeeping and maintenance coordination for hotel teams.
- [Optii](https://www.optiisolutions.com/) — Housekeeping optimization software (now part of Mews).

## Payment Gateways (India)

- [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com) — India's leading payment gateway: UPI, cards, and netbanking with easy PMS/booking-engine integration.
- [PayU](https://payu.in) — Payment gateway with deep Indian market coverage for hotel checkouts.

## Open Source

- [QloApps](https://github.com/Qloapps/QloApps) — The leading open-source hotel PMS (11k+ stars): PMS, booking engine, and hotel website on PHP/MySQL.
- [Kamra PMS](https://github.com/Kamra-PMS/kamra-pms) — Open-source hotel & short-term rental PMS: front desk, booking engine, folios with GST/tax billing, housekeeping, POS, WhatsApp.
- [HAIP](https://github.com/TelivityAI/haip) — Open-source, API-first hotel PMS (TypeScript / Node.js / NestJS).
- [HotelDruid](https://www.hoteldruid.com/) — Free, open-source (AGPL) hotel management: POS, multi-user, and optional hosted booking engine/channel manager.
- [Noblesse Hotel PMS](https://github.com/TheAsilzade/noblesse-hotel-pms) — Full-stack hotel PMS built with NestJS, Next.js, Prisma, and Redis.
- [Hotel PMS (microservices)](https://github.com/Maleesha101/Hotel_PMS) — Cloud-ready hotel PMS built with microservices and REST APIs.
- [Hotel topics on GitHub](https://github.com/topics/hotel-management-system) — 700+ open-source hotel management repos.

## Indian Market

- [MakeMyTrip](https://www.makemytrip.com) — India's largest OTA: the distribution channel Indian hotels can't ignore.
- [Goibibo](https://www.goibibo.com) — Major Indian OTA for hotels and flights (MakeMyTrip group).
- [Yatra](https://www.yatra.com) — Indian OTA with 103,000+ hotels in India and strength in corporate travel.
- [CBIC](https://www.cbic.gov.in) — Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs: the official source for GST notifications and rate changes.

## GST & Billing (India)

- [Hotel GST Invoice Templates](https://github.com/roomnexa-official/hotel-gst-invoice-template) — Free GST-ready invoice templates for Indian hotels: room folios, restaurant bills, B2B invoices (by RoomNexa).
